Projetos de pesquisa

2015 - 2015
Water Matters: A Health Impact Assessment focused on the addition of Water in a Colonia on Presidio TX
Descrição: Multiple health issues are linked to inadequate access to safe water, including gastrointestinal illnesses and ailments such as Giardia infections, dysentery, hepatitis, and dehydration. Improved local water systems may also have indirect health effects through higher property values and tax revenue that support services important to health, such as clinics and social services. The objective of the study is to conduct an HIA focused on water infrastructure. The HIA will help guide a decision by the city of Presidio, Texas, on whether to extend the local water system to the neighboring colonia of Las Pampas..

Transportation Matters: A health impact assessment associated with the public transportation in southern Doña Ana county, New Mexico, United States
Descrição: Public transportation has been linked to positive health outcomes as a result of increased access to health care, opportunities for physical activity, and decreased stress. The objective of the study is to conduct an health impact assessment (HIA) of a project that is being proposed to provide public transportation between Las Cruces and several rural towns/colonias in southern New Mexico along the border. These communities are primarily low resource communities with high rates of poverty and very limited services available locally. The key decision that the HIA would inform is the final routes for the new transit proposal in order to accomplish the most positive impact on the users..

Usos benéficos do biossólido na perspectiva de riscos à saúde pública
Descrição: Consistiu em um estudo sobre o uso do Biossólidos na agricultura.
