Talking to a scientist
Descrição: Price and colleagues at the University College London have created an authentic research experience accessible to year 12 pupils and younger that enhances the science capital of the school pupils and enthuses them to pursue independent reading in the Biosciences. Science capital encompasses the sum of all the science-related knowledge, attitudes, experiences and resources that a pupil has acquired. High levels of science capital are considered a key differentiator in pupils pursuing science study to University level and, we posit, is critical to widening participation (WP) activities to promote university science study in socially and economically disadvantaged students. The Brazilian team at Universidade Federal de Itajubá will conduct a similar project with physics teachers and pre-university school students participating in inquiry-based physics research, at the same time engaging in WP activities. WP activities will take place with local schools, and interviews with teachers and school pupils following the intervention will be recorded for transcription and analysis..
